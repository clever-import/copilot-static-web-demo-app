The Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of our solar system. It is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma, heated to incandescence by nuclear fusion reactions in its core. The Sun is the most important source of energy for life on Earth.

Planets

  • Mercury: The smallest planet and closest to the Sun.
  • Venus: The second planet, known for its thick, toxic atmosphere.
  • Earth: The third planet, home to millions of species, including humans.
  • Mars: The fourth planet, often called the Red Planet.
  • Jupiter: The largest planet in our solar system, known for its Great Red Spot.
  • Saturn: Famous for its stunning rings, it is the sixth planet from the Sun.
  • Uranus: The seventh planet, known for its blue color and unique tilt.
  • Neptune: The eighth planet, known for its deep blue color and strong winds.
  • Pluto: Once considered the ninth planet, now classified as a dwarf planet.
